# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to get their money back for a purchase, made with a note explaining why. It ends the learner's access once approved; there is no separate cancellation. A purchase has at most one Open Refund Request at a time; a new one may be made after a Decline while the purchase still has Refund Eligibility.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation, Pending Chargeback

**Refund**:
The money given back to a learner when a Refund Request is approved. Always the full Refund Amount; there are no partial refunds.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Amount**:
What the learner actually paid for the purchase, after any discount or coupon.
_Avoid_: Refundable amount, list price

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a purchase, counting the whole of the fourteenth day, during which a Refund Request can be made.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation window

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a purchase can still have a Refund Request made against it: inside the Refund Window, under the watch-time and lesson-completion limits, and not a claimed team seat.

**Requested**:
The state of a Refund Request that has been made but not yet decided. A Requested request is **Open**.
_Avoid_: Pending

**Approved**:
The state of a Refund Request that an Admin granted; the Refund is paid. An Approved request is **Resolved**.

**Declined**:
The state of a Refund Request that an Admin turned down; nothing is refunded. A Declined request is **Resolved**.
